# Ship Pack Notes privacy

Ship Pack Notes generates ship packs on your device. The app does not create an account, and it does not send the text you paste to a Ship Pack Notes server. There is no Ship Pack Notes server.

Packs, the free-pack count, and a preview unlock (when RevenueCat is not configured) stay in local storage on the device.

If you buy Unlock Unlimited, Apple or Google processes the payment. RevenueCat receives the purchase so the `unlimited` entitlement can unlock the app. That is the store purchase record, not the text of your feature asks.

To delete local packs, remove the app. To request a refund, use Apple or Google. Ship Pack Notes cannot see your card.
